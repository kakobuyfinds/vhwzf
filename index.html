Before you order

  1. Confirm the product and variant. Open the source listing and check the current price, color and stock for your exact size. If you plan to order through iTaoBuy, check whether it accepts the original merchant URL before paying.
  2. Compare measurements in centimeters. For tops, compare chest width and body length against a similar garment you own. For shoes, use the seller’s chart for the specific listing and check whether it lists foot length or insole length.
  3. Review QC photos if your agent offers them. Compare the received color and size label with your order. For shoes, check both sides and the soles. For clothing, check the print, seams and any zippers. Check the exchange deadline before approving shipment.
  4. Calculate the delivered cost. Add the item price, local delivery, agent fees, international freight and any applicable duties or taxes. Check whether the freight quote uses actual or volumetric weight, especially for shoes shipped with boxes.

Frequently asked questions

What is this iTaoBuy spreadsheet?

It is an independent browsing guide to a selection of real HipobuyFind listings. Use the category filter or search by item number to compare products and open their source pages.

Can I order every item through iTaoBuy?

Compatibility depends on the original merchant link and iTaoBuy’s current purchasing rules. Open the HipobuyFind listing, locate the original merchant link, and confirm support with iTaoBuy for that exact item.

Are the listed prices live?

The cards show a dated snapshot. Check the source listing for the current variant price and review the final quote with your agent before paying.
